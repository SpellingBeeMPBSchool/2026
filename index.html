📘 Page guide Learn the modes, filters, BETA voice spelling, progress and extra tools

Use this quick guide to get the most out of your Spelling Bee training.

1. Start here

  1. Choose Category A or Category B.
  2. Select a numbered tile to open that word.
  3. Use the search box to find a word by number or by text.

🟡 View mode

Use this mode to review the word list. Open a tile to see the word, listen to its pronunciation, move to the previous or next word, change audio speed, or save a word with Star.

🌌 Practice mode

Listen and spell the word with the on-screen or physical keyboard, then press Check. Use Hint for help, Blind for a greater challenge, Reveal to show the answer, or Retry to try again.

2. Progress colours and filters

  • Green: answered correctly in regular Practice.
  • Galaxy purple: answered correctly with Blind enabled.
  • Red: needs another review.
  • Blue: the answer was revealed.
  • Dark: not completed yet.

Use the filter chips in Practice mode to show all, pending, review, mastered, short, long or starred words.

3. Extra tools

  • Random word: opens a word for a quick session.
  • Contest round: simulates a round of 5 or 10 words.
  • Alphabet guide: select a letter to hear its English name.
  • My Stats: review your session results and category progress.
  • SFX: turn sound effects on or off.

4. Shortcuts and voice spelling (BETA)

Letters enter a spelling, Backspace deletes a letter, Enter checks the answer, Space plays the word, and the ←/→ keys move between words.

The microphone spelling feature is in BETA and may not work reliably. Press Voice spelling · BETA, then say English letter names one at a time: A (“ay”), B (“bee”), C (“see”). Leave a short pause between letters and watch the status text to see what was captured. You can say delete, clear, listen, reveal or check. If the browser combines the spelling into the exact target word, the page can fill the remaining letters. Repeated final results are ignored within a session, and the microphone is reconnected after a pause when the browser allows it.

MICROPHONE SPELLING IS IN BETA and may not work well: it can miss, merge or mishear spoken letters. It depends on the browser's speech service, microphone permissions, network and pronunciation; identical results cannot be guaranteed across devices. Use HTTPS or localhost and allow microphone access. Current Chrome or Edge may work better; Brave may have its speech service disabled. The on-screen and physical keyboards are the reliable alternatives.

Your practice progress and starred words are saved in this browser separately for each category. Session statistics reset when the tab session ends.
